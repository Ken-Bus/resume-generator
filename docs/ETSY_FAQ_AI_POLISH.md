## Recommended FAQ entry

**Q: What counts as one of my 5 generations?**

A: Your access code includes 5 AI generations. A generation is used when the AI successfully builds or improves your resume. Typing, previewing, editing, switching templates and downloading never use one.

**Q: If the AI Polish doesn't change my resume, do I lose a generation?**

A: No. When you choose "Polish My Resume with AI," the AI reviews your resume. A generation is used only if it makes meaningful improvements to your wording. If your answers are already clear and it finds nothing meaningful to improve, nothing is used, and your resume stays exactly as you wrote it. (Fixing only capital letters, punctuation or spacing doesn't count as an improvement.)

**Q: What if something goes wrong while the AI is working?**

A: If the AI can't finish, nothing is used. Your counter always shows how many generations you have left.

**Q: Can I keep asking the AI to review the same resume?**

A: If the AI has reviewed your resume several times in a row and found nothing to improve, the app will ask you to add or change some of your answers (or wait a few minutes) before trying again. This protects your account and is never charged.

## Short version (for the description's "HOW IT WORKS" section, one line)

"AI Polish reviews your wording and uses one of your 5 generations only when it makes meaningful improvements; if there's nothing to improve, nothing is used."

## Notes for the owner
- Do not promise a number of "improvements" or "guaranteed rewrites". The AI is told to leave already-clear lines alone, so a strong first draft may get no changes, and that is by design.
- This wording does not change the price, the five-generation entitlement, or how Tailor works (Tailor stays free for up to 5 runs per paid generation).
- Per the shop's listing rules, never name the underlying AI model.
