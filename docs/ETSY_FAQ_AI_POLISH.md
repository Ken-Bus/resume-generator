## Recommended FAQ entries

**Q: What counts as one of my 5 generations?**

A: Your access code includes 5 AI generations. Analyzing a pasted resume uses 1, and every analysis comes with **one AI Polish included**: your first AI Polish after an analysis uses no generation. After that, each AI Polish uses 1 generation, but only when it makes real improvements (it rewrites some of your wording or corrects three or more mistakes). If you build your resume step by step instead of pasting one, there is no analysis, so your first meaningful Polish uses 1 generation. Typing, building your resume step by step, previewing, editing, switching templates, tailoring to a job and downloading never use one. The page tells you what a step will use before you start it.

**Q: What does "Analyze Resume" do? Will it fix my spelling?**

A: Analyze reads the resume you paste and sorts it into the editor fields (name, jobs, education, skills and so on). It keeps your spelling and wording exactly as you wrote it, so it does not correct typos or grammar. After it finishes you can fix anything yourself for free, or use the AI Polish that came with your analysis to correct spelling and grammar and tidy the wording.

**Q: Do I pay twice to Analyze and then Polish?**

A: No. Your first AI Polish after an Analyze is included. It is used up only when the AI makes real improvements: if it finds nothing, or only one or two small fixes, it applies those and nothing is used. Your included Polish never expires, and you can use it even when you have no generations left. Each later meaningful Polish uses 1 generation.

**Q: What if I don't like what the AI Polish changed?**

A: You can restore your own wording: one part at a time, or all of it. Restoring does not give back the included Polish or the generation, because the AI did the work. A second meaningful Polish uses another generation.

**Q: What happens when I use my last generation?**

A: When you have used all your generations and your included AI Polish, you have **four days** to finish: you can keep editing, switching templates, previewing and downloading your Word and PDF files. After four days the code stops working. If you still have an included AI Polish, the four days start when you use it. Four days of access is not four days of storage: please download both your Word and PDF files and save them on your computer, a USB drive or another safe place.

**Q: Is my resume stored online?**

A: No. Your finished resume is not permanently stored on our servers. Your draft is saved automatically in your own browser on your own device (for up to 14 days), so it may not be there if you change computers or clear your browser data. Only the AI's last Analyze or Polish result is kept, encrypted, for 24 hours so you can recover it if the page closes. Download your Word and PDF files to keep your work.

**Q: If the AI Polish doesn't improve my resume, do I lose a generation?**

A: No. When you choose "Polish My Resume with AI," the AI reviews your resume and corrects spelling, grammar and tense, tidies run-on lines and repeats, and says things more clearly, using only what you wrote. It uses your included Polish (or 1 generation if you have no included Polish left) when it rewrites some of your wording or corrects three or more mistakes. If it finds fewer than that (for example just one or two small fixes), it still applies those fixes and nothing is used. If it finds nothing to improve, or if everything it suggested added details you didn't give, nothing is changed and nothing is used. You can always undo a polish with "Restore my wording."

**Q: Will the AI add things I didn't say?**

A: It is designed not to. It may only reword what you wrote, in plain language that fits you. Your name, contact details, employers, job titles, dates, schools and certifications are always copied exactly from your answers. If the AI suggests something that adds a detail you didn't give (for example a number, who benefited, a result, a skill you didn't list, or a bigger role than you described), that suggestion is left out and your own wording is kept. You can always undo a polish with "Restore my wording," and you should still read your resume before you send it. No tool can promise it never makes a mistake.

**Q: Will it make me sound like someone I'm not?**

A: No. The AI is told to keep your level of involvement ("helped" stays "helped"), to use natural, modest words, and not to add praise such as "motivated" or "dedicated" unless you wrote it. Your resume should sound like you on a good day, not like a different person. Always read it over and change anything that doesn't sound like you.

**Q: What is "Tailor to This Job"?**

A: Paste a job description and the AI adjusts the emphasis and wording of your resume toward it, using only what is already in your resume. Each time you use a generation you also get up to 5 tailoring runs for that resume, and tailoring never uses a generation. It follows the same rules as AI Polish: suggestions that add details you didn't give are left out.

**Q: What if something goes wrong while the AI is working?**

A: If the AI can't finish, nothing is used. Your counter always shows how many generations you have left. If your screen refreshes or your phone loses signal while the AI is working, your resume is still there when the page comes back, and pressing the same button again for the same resume recovers that step instead of using a second generation. Within a day, the app tells you plainly when it has prepared a fresh version of your result (the wording may differ from the first one). If you change your resume and press the button again, that is a new request, and the app tells you so before anything else happens. If the AI service itself is very busy you will be told to wait a minute and try again. If a generation ever goes missing, message us with the reference code shown on the screen (it looks like R-1A2B3C4D). Never send your access code or your resume.

**Q: Is my resume kept if I refresh the page, close the browser, or lose power?**

A: Yes. While you work, the app saves your draft automatically in your own browser, on your own device, a moment after you stop typing. If you refresh, close the browser, or your device shuts off, the app offers "Continue where I left off" the next time you open it (or "Start fresh" if you would rather not). Saving the draft never uses an AI generation. If power is lost, the last few seconds of typing may not have been saved. Your draft is never uploaded, it is deleted after 14 days, and it does not follow you to another phone or computer. It is also removed if you clear your browser data. On a shared or public computer, use "Clear this saved copy" when you are done. Your access code is never kept in the draft.

**Q: I used a generation but lost the result (the page closed, my phone died). Do I lose it?**

A: No. When an AI Analyze or AI Polish generation finishes, the app keeps one encrypted copy of that result for up to 24 hours so you can get it back. Open the app on the same device or a different one, enter the same access code you bought with, and choose "Restore my result." Getting it back does not use another generation. (AI Tailor results are not kept this way; Tailor is already included with each generation.) The copy is encrypted before it is stored and can be opened only with your access code. Our systems stop returning it after 24 hours and a cleanup job deletes it. Because of how server backups work, a scrambled (encrypted) copy may remain in a backup for a short time after that, and it cannot be read or restored in the app. We do not keep your original pasted resume, or the instructions sent to the AI, for this feature.

**Q: Can I use a very long resume?**

A: AI steps work best on resumes of up to about 5 to 6 pages. Pasting more than about 20,000 characters is not accepted for analysis, and a resume too long for AI Polish or Tailor is refused with a message before anything is used. You can still edit and download any length yourself.

**Q: What if I don't give the AI much to work with?**

A: It will tell you. If there are only a few words to work with, it asks you to add a little more (what you did, and what you worked with) instead of making something up, and nothing is used.

**Q: Can I keep asking the AI to review the same resume?**

A: If the AI has reviewed your resume several times in a row and found nothing worth applying, the app will ask you to wait a few minutes before trying again (you can keep editing and downloading meanwhile). This protects your account and is never charged.

**Q: Are my resume and access code kept private?**

A: Your resume text is sent to the AI service to be processed and to our server to build your PDF or Word file. We do not write your resume or your access code to our logs. Two things are kept, both described above: an automatic draft in your own browser on your own device (never uploaded; deleted after 14 days), and, only after an AI Analyze or AI Polish generation is used, one encrypted copy of that result on our server for up to 24 hours so you can get it back. Encrypted copies may remain in system backups for a short time after they expire and cannot be read there. Never send your access code or your resume to us in a message.

## Short version (for the description's "HOW IT WORKS" section, one line)

"Your code includes 5 AI generations. Analyzing a resume uses 1 and includes one free AI Polish; after that, a Polish uses 1 only when it makes real improvements. Editing, previewing and downloading are free, and after your last AI step you have 4 days to finish and download."

## Claims to avoid (not demonstrated, so not to be made)
- Do not say the resume is "ATS-friendly", "ATS-optimized" or "will get you interviews/hired". The PDF text is real, selectable text (tested), but nothing here has been tested against any applicant tracking system, and outcomes depend on the applicant and the employer.
- Do not promise a number of improvements, "guaranteed rewrites", or that the AI "never" makes mistakes.
- Do not name the underlying AI model (shop listing rule).
- Do not describe the free sample as an example of what the AI will write for a customer: it is a fictional warehouse/cashier resume (with an executive "Office Sample"), not an AI result.
- Do not say your resume is "saved", "stored" or "backed up" online. It is not. Four days is how long the code still signs in and downloads, not how long anything is kept.
